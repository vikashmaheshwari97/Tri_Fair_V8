Attached paper assessment

  • FAPO: Fully Automated Prompt Optimization of Multi-Step LLM Pipelines: Adopt failure attribution, scoped revisions and iteration memory; preserve the fixed inference pipeline.
  • Fairness Aware Reward Optimization: Reward-model training with fairness constraints. Theoretical guarantees do not transfer to this discrete prompt optimizer; do not claim FARO guarantees.
  • MO-CAPO: Multi-Objective Cost-Aware Prompt Optimization: Retain token-weighted deployment cost and progressive common-block intensification.

Research direction

Jointly optimize task quality, deployment input/output-token cost and group unfairness, with bounded and auditable prompt search. This is a proposed direction, not a claim of established novelty or guaranteed dominance.

Deployment cost is the configured weighted mean of input and output tokens. Downstream search/audit tokens and meta-generation tokens are reported separately. HPC charges are a separate resource measure.

Implemented upgrade

  • New versioned AttributedTriFairOptimizer; old v8/v8.2 and baseline implementations preserved.
  • Cached search-only confusion, raw-format and conditional-error attribution, with explicit support counts.
  • JSON proposal plus brief failure diagnosis; concrete class-answer and embedded-example rejection.
  • Every proposal counts toward operator attempts; retain bounded histories of rejected and measured proposals.
  • A measured revision may have zero shots or one complete short gold-labeled example from the frozen few-shot split.
  • Quality revisions may include an explicit allowed-label catalog; additional deployment tokens are charged normally.
  • Common-block uncertainty-aware racing and archive scoring inherited unchanged.
  • Method-level generation/idle bounds, plus a common three-idle-step watchdog and 200-step pilot ceiling for all methods.
  • New pilot enables label-choice grammar for all methods, meta output cap 512, identical 1M downstream ceiling including 35% final-audit reserve.
  • New v83 results namespace, decoding metadata comparison, progress logging and resumable optimizer memory.

Algorithm

Initialize a statistically ready search archive from the supplied seeds.
While the search token cap and step/call limits permit:
    Select complementary parents and an objective-directed operator.
    Count the proposal attempt, even if its output will be rejected.
    Attribute cached search errors; include measured/rejected revision memory.
    Generate a bounded JSON revision with a reproducible, changing batch seed.
    Reject invalid, duplicate, overlong, example-bearing or fixed-answer instructions.
    Construct zero-shot and optional frozen-pool gold-demonstration candidates.
    Race candidates on common group-complete blocks using existing metric gates.
    Store actual measured outcomes and advance unresolved incumbent coverage.
    Stop after repeated rounds without new downstream evaluations.
Freeze up to two candidates before the common final search audit.
Complete their search-set coverage within the original total token cap.
Report quality, accuracy, cost and unfairness from the same selected prompt.
Then freeze settings, validate, and evaluate test data separately.

Validation and limits

  • Local deterministic fake-model tests validate code behavior, not research performance.
  • The vLLM label-choice backend requires GPU smoke validation; an explicit all-method opt-out exists.
  • Conditional-error attribution is heuristic diagnostic evidence, not proof of a causal fairness mechanism.
  • The label-choice decoding protocol differs from archived unconstrained results; comparisons require fresh runs of every method.
  • The pilot is a one-seed search-split screen with a token ceiling, not a held-out or significance claim.
  • No identity-swap dataset augmentation, learned reward-model training, Bayesian surrogate, or unconstrained pipeline/code rewriting is claimed as implemented.
  • All three objectives may conflict; improvement and dominance remain empirical questions.
